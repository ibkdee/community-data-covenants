# Scope and safeguards

This is an AI-assisted concept-stage research artefact, not a clinical service or secure deployment. All scenarios are synthetic. The decision model assumes trusted inputs; it does not implement authentication, a network gateway, offline synchronisation or storage. The separate experiment models expiry policies, not the decision model's one-minute lease and five-minute freshness limit. It cannot validate that model's security.

The simulation assumes a single outage with an initially valid cached permission and a possible withdrawal unknown to the institution until reconnection. It compares request-level errors, not patient outcomes. Outage lengths and withdrawal probabilities are stress-test assumptions, not Nigerian field estimates. No actual community engagement or host partnership is claimed. Data already exported or incorporated into models cannot be recalled by this design.

Future work requires participatory governance design, legal and ethics review, independent security assessment, actual synchronisation and access enforcement, and evaluation of participant-level harms and exclusion. Individual refusal must not be overridden by collective approval. Healthcare access must never depend on consenting to secondary research.

Prior art to examine includes W3C ODRL (https://www.w3.org/TR/odrl-model/), GA4GH Data Use Ontology (https://www.ga4gh.org/product/data-use-ontology-duo/), and dynamic consent systems. No claim of unprecedented invention is made.
